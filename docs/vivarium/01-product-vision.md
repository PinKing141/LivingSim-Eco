# 01 - Product Vision

## Product definition

LivingSim: Vivarium is an observer-first artificial-life ecosystem simulation. A world is created from a deterministic seed, populated with living creatures and resources, and then allowed to develop without direct player intervention.

The entertainment comes from watching survival, competition, migration, reproduction, ecological collapse, recovery, and evolution emerge from interacting systems.

The project is closer to a **living nature aquarium / procedural nature documentary** than a conventional strategy or management game.

## Design pillars

### Observation over control

The player studies the ecosystem rather than solving it. The primary interaction is deciding what to watch and investigate, not what to change.

### Emergence over scripting

Memorable events should arise from interacting simulation systems rather than authored story beats.

### Deep time matters

The simulation should remain interesting at three scales:

- individual lives,
- generations and populations,
- centuries or longer periods of ecological history.

### Evolution must be readable

Genetic change should not exist only as hidden numbers. Players should be able to see how populations are changing and understand the environmental pressures behind those changes.

### Every seed tells a history

A deterministic seed should create a repeatable starting world. Seeds become interesting not only because of geography but because of the natural histories they produce.

### Simulation and presentation stay separate

The ecosystem must be able to run without a graphical renderer. Rendering, UI, camera systems, and visual effects are observers of simulation state rather than dependencies of it.

## Explicitly not the game

The baseline game is:

- **not** a zoo or aquarium management game,
- **not** a god game built around spawning, killing, rescuing, or feeding creatures,
- **not** a survival game played as one animal,
- **not** a traditional strategy game with a victory condition,
- **not** committed to any final visual style yet.

## Core promise

The game should repeatedly create the feeling:

> I did not script that. I just watched nature produce it.

The reward is discovering **what happened in this world** and understanding why.
